# About

I am a pharmacist and researcher at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. My work examines the validity and reproducibility of preclinical research, with particular attention to the pathway from experimental findings to clinical application.

My research combines meta-research, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, replication studies, and open science practices. I also have a background in neuropharmacology and behavioral models using zebrafish and rodents.

I contribute to collaborative initiatives focused on research transparency, methodological robustness, evidence synthesis, and the improvement of biomedical research practices.

# Research

## Meta-research and research integrity

Examining how study design, reporting practices, analytical choices, and research systems influence the reliability of scientific findings.

## Translational validity

Investigating the conditions associated with successful translation from preclinical experiments to clinical research and practice.

## Preclinical evidence synthesis

Using systematic reviews and advanced meta-analytic methods to evaluate evidence from animal and experimental research.

## Reproducibility and open science

Developing and evaluating practices that strengthen transparency, replicability, data sharing, and methodological robustness.
