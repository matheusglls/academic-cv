About

Research at the preclinical–clinical interface.

I am a pharmacist and researcher at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. My work examines the validity and reproducibility of preclinical research, with particular attention to the pathway from experimental findings to clinical application.

My research combines meta-research, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, replication studies, and open science practices. I also have a background in neuropharmacology and behavioral models using zebrafish and rodents.

I contribute to collaborative initiatives focused on research transparency, methodological robustness, evidence synthesis, and the improvement of biomedical research practices.

Research

I investigate the reliability and translational value of preclinical evidence.

My current work uses systematic reviews, meta-analysis, replication studies, and meta-research to examine how experimental design, analytical decisions, and reporting practices shape biomedical findings.

This work spans research integrity, reproducibility, animal models of psychiatric disorders, and the preclinical–clinical research pathway.
