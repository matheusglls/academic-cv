About

I am a pharmacist, PhD in Pharmacology and Therapeutics, and researcher at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. My work examines the validity, reproducibility, and translation of preclinical research.

My research combines meta-research, systematic reviews, meta-analysis, replication studies, and open science practices. I also have a background in neuropharmacology and behavioral models using zebrafish and rodents.

I contribute to collaborative initiatives focused on research transparency, methodological robustness, evidence synthesis, and the improvement of biomedical research practices.
